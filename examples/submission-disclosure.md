# Benchmark submission disclosure

- Submitter, affiliations and relationship to engine/steward:
- Source commit, evaluator hash, package, policy/profile/pack hashes:
- Engine, adapter, configuration and executable/library artifacts:
- Workload/label provenance/license, evidence class and prior exposure:
- Model/revision/quantization, runtime versions and artifacts:
- Prompt/decoder/seed/budgets/cache and startup treatment:
- Hardware, OS, load, isolation profile and probe evidence:
- Duration, concurrency, engine order and cadence:
- First-attempt inventory, failures, unsupported operations and unknown usage:
- Components, raw denominators, family/domain counts and gates:
- Token metric, whole-pipeline gaps and selected baseline:
- Latency boundary, resource coverage and excluded transient peaks:
- Uncertainty method and correlated samples:
- Sealed raw identity and restricted independent review procedure:
- Sanitized export identity and redaction review:
- Matching-environment replay and reproduction report:
- Proposed claims, reviews completed and approvals outstanding:

Submitter-completed metadata is not independent execution attestation.
